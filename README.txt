WHO DOES WHAT? — 12-TASK MOBILE PROTOTYPE

Medical Officer: OPD clinical services; Provide treatment; Lead UPHC team
Staff Nurse: Assess patient's condition; Immunization and counselling services; Follow-up services
ANM: Services in outreach camps like RI and UHSND; Health promotion activities; Record maintenance
ASHA: Mobilize the community; Home visits; Awareness in the community

The Follow-up Services card was created to complete the supplied 12-card set because no separate artwork was included in the uploaded files.
The game is mobile-first, touch-friendly, randomly shuffles cards, supports drag/drop and tap-to-select, and includes anonymous analytics event hooks.
